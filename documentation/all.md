## KA-01: Maintenance Request — How to Submit and What to Expect

| Field | Value |

| **URL Name** | `maintenance-request-how-to-submit` |

| **Channels** | Customer, Internal |
| **Data Category** | Maintenance |

**Answer (paste into the rich text Answer field):**

---

**Submitting a Maintenance Request**

Pacific Haven Properties offers three ways to report a maintenance issue:

1. **Tenant Portal Chat** — Chat with the Tenant Concierge agent 24/7. The agent will collect your issue details and automatically create a maintenance case for you.
2. **Online Portal** — Log in to your tenant portal, navigate to **Maintenance**, and click **New Request**.
3. **Property Management Office** — Call or visit your local office during business hours (Monday–Friday, 8 AM–8 PM).

---

**Maintenance Priority Categories**

| Priority | Examples | Target Response |
|---|---|---|
| **Emergency** | No heat in winter, gas leak, flood, no running water, electrical hazard | Contact the 24-hour Emergency Hotline immediately — do NOT use the portal |
| **High** | Broken lock, HVAC failure, major appliance outage | Within 24 hours |
| **Medium** | Leaking faucet, broken window (non-emergency), pest issue | Within 3 business days |
| **Low** | Minor cosmetic repairs, light bulb replacement, non-urgent requests | Within 7 business days |

---

**Emergency Hotline**

For life-safety emergencies (gas leak, fire, flooding, no heat below 50°F outside), **do not use the portal**. Call the Pacific Haven Emergency Hotline directly. The Tenant Concierge will provide you with the hotline number and will also log a High Priority case on your behalf.

---

**What Happens After You Submit**

- You will receive a confirmation with a Case number.
- A maintenance technician will contact you within the target response window to schedule access.
- You will be notified when the case is marked **Resolved**.
- If your issue is not resolved within the target window, reply to your case confirmation to escalate.

---

**Tenant Access Requirements**

You must provide access to your unit for scheduled maintenance. If you are unavailable, contact your property office to reschedule. Pacific Haven staff will not enter your unit without a scheduled appointment except in emergencies.

**Answer:**

---

**Guest Policy at Pacific Haven Properties**

Pacific Haven welcomes guests in our communities. The following rules apply to all properties to ensure a comfortable environment for all residents.

---

**Overnight Guests**

- Guests may stay overnight without prior notice for up to **7 consecutive nights**.
- Guests may stay no more than **14 nights total in any 30-day period**.
- Guests staying beyond these limits must be registered as an **Extended Guest** (see below) or added to the lease as an occupant.

---

**Extended Guest Registration**

If a guest will stay for more than 7 consecutive nights or 14 nights in a month, the tenant must notify the Property Management Office in writing before the 8th consecutive night. The property management team will review and approve or deny the extended stay. Approval is not automatic and may be denied if it would exceed the unit's occupancy limit.

---

**Guest Conduct**

- Guests are subject to all community rules while on property.
- Tenants are responsible for the conduct of their guests.
- Guests may not use community amenities (gym, pool, rooftop) unless accompanied by the registered tenant.
- Guests may not be given access to community gate codes or building access codes. A registered tenant must accompany the guest.
- Guest vehicles must be registered with the office and parked in designated visitor spaces only.

---

**Violations**

Repeated guest policy violations — including unauthorized long-term occupants — are grounds for a lease violation notice and may lead to lease termination proceedings.

---

**Keyfob and Building Access Devices**

All Pacific Haven tenants receive one building access keyfob upon move-in at no charge. Replacement keyfobs are available at a fee.

---

**Replacement Keyfob Fee**

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Replacement keyfob | **$75 per keyfob** |
| Additional keyfob (extra copy) | $75 per keyfob |
| Temporary access card (lost keyfob, pending replacement) | No charge, valid for 7 days |

---

**How to Order a Replacement Keyfob**

1. Chat with the **Tenant Concierge** in the portal — the agent will quote the $75 fee and confirm your order once you accept.
2. Alternatively, visit the Property Management Office in person.
3. Payment is charged to your account and will appear on your next monthly statement.
4. Replacement keyfobs are typically programmed and available for pickup within **1 business day**.

---

**Lost or Stolen Keyfob**

- Report a lost or stolen keyfob to the Property Management Office **immediately**.
- Lost keyfobs will be **deactivated within 2 hours** of your report to prevent unauthorized access.
- You will be issued a temporary access card (no charge) while your replacement is being prepared.
- If your keyfob is stolen as part of a break-in or other crime, notify local law enforcement first and provide the incident report number to the office.

---

**Digital Door Unlock (SmartRent)**

If your unit is equipped with SmartRent smart locks, you can request a temporary door unlock through the Tenant Concierge chat. This requires identity verification via a one-time passcode sent to your registered phone number. The digital unlock does not replace your physical keyfob.

## KA-05: Lease Renewal — Process, Timeline, and Options

| Field | Value |

| **URL Name** | `lease-renewal-process-timeline` |
| **Summary** | How lease renewals work at Pacific Haven Properties, including when you will be contacted, your renewal options, rent adjustment policies, and what happens if you choose not to renew. |
| **Channels** | Customer, Internal |
| **Data Category** | Lease Policies |

**Answer:**

---

**Lease Renewal at Pacific Haven Properties**

Pacific Haven makes lease renewal straightforward. Here is everything you need to know about the renewal process.

---

**When Will You Hear From Us?**

- Pacific Haven will contact you **60 days before your lease expiration date** with your renewal offer.
- You will receive the offer by email and through the Tenant Portal.
- You must respond to the renewal offer within **30 days of receipt**.

---

**Your Renewal Options**

| Option | Details |
|---|---|
| **Renew for 12 months** | Standard renewal term. Rent adjustment applied per market rate review. |
| **Renew for 6 months** | Short-term renewal available at a premium rate (typically 5–10% above 12-month rate). |
| **Month-to-month** | Available after initial lease term. Charged at a month-to-month premium. 30-day written notice required to vacate. |
| **Non-renewal (move out)** | You must provide written notice of non-renewal 30 days before lease expiration. Move-out inspection will be scheduled. |

---

**Rent Adjustment Policy**

Rent for renewals is set based on current market rates for your property and unit type. Pacific Haven is committed to fair, consistent pricing. Rent adjustments are based solely on market conditions and payment history. Any increase will be clearly stated in your renewal offer before you are asked to decide.

---

**What Happens If You Don't Respond?**

If Pacific Haven does not receive a response within 30 days of the renewal offer, your lease will automatically convert to **month-to-month** at the month-to-month premium rate on the first day after your lease expires.

---

**Move-Out Process (Non-Renewal)**

- Submit written notice of non-renewal to the Property Management Office.
- A move-out inspection will be scheduled for the last day of your tenancy or within 24 hours after.
- Your security deposit will be returned, minus any deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear, within 21 days of move-out (California) or 31 days (Oregon and Washington), per applicable state law.

## KA-06: Maintenance — Appliance and HVAC Troubleshooting Guide

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Common Appliance and HVAC Issues — Tenant Troubleshooting Guide |
| **URL Name** | `appliance-hvac-troubleshooting-guide` |
| **Summary** | Self-service troubleshooting steps for the most common appliance and HVAC issues reported at Pacific Haven properties, helping tenants resolve simple problems before submitting a maintenance request. |
| **Channels** | Customer, Internal |
| **Data Category** | Maintenance |

**Answer:**

---

**Try These Steps Before Submitting a Request**

Many common issues can be resolved quickly without waiting for a technician. Work through the relevant steps below first. If the issue persists, submit a maintenance request through the Tenant Portal or chat with the Tenant Concierge.

---

**Heating or Cooling Not Working**

1. Check your thermostat — confirm it is set to the correct mode (Heat or Cool) and the target temperature is above (for heat) or below (for cool) the current room temperature.
2. Check the circuit breaker — locate your unit's breaker panel and confirm the HVAC breaker is in the **On** position. Reset it once if tripped.
3. Check the air filter — a clogged air filter will cause the system to shut off. Filters should be replaced every 90 days. Replacement filters are available at the property office at no charge.
4. Check all vents — confirm supply and return vents in your unit are fully open and not blocked by furniture.
5. If none of the above resolves the issue, submit a **High Priority** maintenance request. If outside temperature is below 50°F and heat is not restored, escalate to **Emergency**.

---

**No Hot Water**

1. Check the water heater circuit breaker or gas valve (depending on unit type).
2. If electric: reset the breaker once.
3. If gas: confirm the pilot light is lit (check the label on the unit for re-lighting instructions).
4. Allow 30 minutes for the water to reheat after a reset.
5. If hot water is not restored, submit a **High Priority** maintenance request.

---

**Dishwasher Not Draining**

1. Check the drain filter — remove and clean the filter at the bottom of the dishwasher tub.
2. Check that the garbage disposal (if present) is clear — a clogged disposal blocks the dishwasher drain.
3. Run the disposal briefly, then run the dishwasher.
4. If not resolved, submit a **Medium Priority** maintenance request.

---

**Garbage Disposal Not Working**

1. Press the **red reset button** on the bottom of the disposal unit (under the sink).
2. Insert the hex key (Allen wrench, usually stored under the sink) into the centre bottom socket and turn back and forth to free any jam.
3. Run water and turn the disposal on.
4. If not resolved, submit a **Medium Priority** maintenance request.

---

**Circuit Breaker Tripping Repeatedly**

- A breaker that trips once and stays on after a reset is normal.
- A breaker that trips repeatedly indicates an overloaded circuit or a faulty appliance.
- Unplug appliances on that circuit one at a time to identify the cause.
- **Do not** use extension cords as permanent wiring.
- If you cannot identify the cause or the panel is hot to the touch, submit an **Emergency** maintenance request immediately.

| **Channels** | Customer, Internal |
| **Data Category** | Community Guidelines |

**Answer:**

---

**Community Rules at Pacific Haven Properties**

Following these community guidelines helps ensure a comfortable experience for all residents. Violations may result in a written warning, fines, or lease termination proceedings depending on severity and frequency.

---

**Parking**

- Each unit is assigned one parking space, noted in your lease agreement.
- Additional vehicle parking may be available at an additional monthly fee — contact your property office.
- Visitor parking is available in designated visitor spaces only (marked **V**). Visitors may park for a maximum of **48 consecutive hours**.
- Vehicles parked in non-designated spaces or blocking fire lanes will be towed at the owner's expense without notice.
- All vehicles (tenant and visitor) must be registered with the property office. Electric vehicle charging stations are available in designated EV spaces only.

---

**Noise and Quiet Hours**

| Day | Quiet Hours |
|---|---|
| Sunday – Thursday | 10:00 PM – 8:00 AM |
| Friday – Saturday | 11:00 PM – 9:00 AM |

- Televisions, music, and gatherings must be kept at a reasonable volume within quiet hours.
- Excessive noise complaints from neighbours will result in a written warning after the first incident and a lease violation notice after the second.
- Construction and renovation work inside units is prohibited without prior written approval and is restricted to Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–5 PM only.

---

**Pool (Where Available)**

- Pool hours: 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily.
- Residents must show their keyfob to access the pool area.
- Guests must be accompanied by a registered tenant at all times.
- No glass containers in the pool area.
- Children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult tenant.
- Maximum 4 guests per resident at the pool simultaneously.

---

**Fitness Centre (Where Available)**

- Open 24 hours, keyfob access required.
- No outside guests in the fitness centre.
- Wipe down all equipment after use.
- Pacific Haven is not responsible for personal injury or loss of belongings in the fitness centre.

---

**Bicycle Storage**

- Designated bicycle storage rooms are available on the ground floor of each building.
- Bicycles must be registered with the property office (free of charge).
- Bicycles may **not** be stored on balconies, in hallways, or in stairwells.
- Pacific Haven is not responsible for theft from bicycle storage. Residents are encouraged to use a secondary lock.

---

**Package Management**

- Pacific Haven properties use a secure package locker system.
- You will receive an access code by email or text when a package is deposited.
- Packages unclaimed for more than 7 days will be moved to the property office for pickup.
- Pacific Haven is not responsible for packages delivered outside the locker system.

---

**Recycling and Waste**

- All properties participate in the city recycling programme.
- Recycling bins are clearly labelled in the waste room on each floor.
- Bulk item disposal (furniture, appliances) must be arranged through the property office — **do not** leave bulk items in common areas or near dumpsters.
- Hazardous waste (batteries, paint, electronics) must be disposed of at a certified drop-off location.
